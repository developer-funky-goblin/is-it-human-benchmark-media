- **Seasonal Affective Disorder (SAD)**: A form of depression tied to reduced sunlight exposure during fall and winter months.
- **Reduced Sunlight and Vitamin D**: Less daylight lowers vitamin D synthesis, which is linked to mood regulation.
- **Disrupted Circadian Rhythms**: Shorter days can throw off the body's internal clock, affecting sleep and energy levels.
- **Serotonin Fluctuations**: Sunlight exposure influences serotonin production; less light can mean lower serotonin.
- **Melatonin Overproduction**: Longer nights can increase melatonin, contributing to lethargy and low mood.
- **Social Isolation**: Cold weather often reduces outdoor activity and social contact, compounding low mood.
- **Holiday Stress**: Financial and social pressures around the winter holidays can add to seasonal sadness.
