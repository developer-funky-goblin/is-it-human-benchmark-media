Here are nine tips to help you succeed in your first days at a new job:

1. **Establish Clear Goals and Objectives:** Meet with your manager early on to understand expectations, priorities, and how your success will be measured.
2. **Introduce Yourself:** Take the initiative to meet colleagues across teams, not just your immediate group, to build a broad network early.
3. **Learn the Company Culture:** Observe communication norms, meeting etiquette, and unwritten rules before trying to change anything.
4. **Ask Questions:** Don't be afraid to ask for clarification -- it's expected and better than guessing incorrectly.
5. **Take Notes:** Document processes, names, and decisions as you learn them; you won't remember everything.
6. **Understand the Tools:** Get comfortable with the software, systems, and workflows your team relies on daily.
7. **Find a Mentor:** Identify someone experienced who can help you navigate both the work and the culture.
8. **Manage Your Time:** Prioritize ruthlessly in the early weeks when everything feels urgent.
9. **Be Patient with Yourself:** Competence takes time to build; don't expect to feel fully capable in the first month.

Following these steps will help you build a strong foundation and set yourself up for long-term success in your new role.
