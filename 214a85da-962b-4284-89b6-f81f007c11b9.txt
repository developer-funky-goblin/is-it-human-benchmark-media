A corollary to this is, when reading books, mark them up! Don't be precious with them. Destroy the spine so it's easier to read, fold pages, write the important stuff inside the front cover of the book. Cross out stuff you disagree with, draw a big circles around the stuff that resonates. Not only does it make consumption a lot more engaging, it makes a revisit of that book extremely rewarding. Don't get caught up trying to figure out the right notation or highlighter color for a line. Just mark them up and later it'll make sense.
