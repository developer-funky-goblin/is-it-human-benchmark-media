Haha, damn right -- **Safety Last!** (1923) is an absolute banger, and that clock scene still holds up as one of the most insane practical stunts ever put on film. Harold Lloyd did that himself, missing fingers and all. Silent-era stunt work was genuinely unhinged in a way modern action movies can't touch, because there was no compositing to fall back on -- if it looked real, it's because it was real.
