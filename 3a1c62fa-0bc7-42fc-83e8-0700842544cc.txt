INT. McBain's Garage - DAY

Rick pulls a bottle of blue liquid from a shelf, testing it against a car battery. Morty leans in the doorway, arms crossed.

MORTY: Rick, you said this was a two minute job.

RICK: (not looking up) It *is* a two minute job, Morty, if you don't keep interrupting me every fourteen seconds with your -- (gestures vaguely) -- anxiety noises.

MORTY: I'm not making anxiety noises!

RICK: You're radiating them, Morty. It's a whole frequency. (holds bottle up to light) Ah, there we go.

He pours a few drops into the battery. It hums, then glows faint blue.

MORTY: Is that -- is that supposed to happen?

RICK: Everything's supposed to happen, Morty, that's what makes it happen. (beat) Now get in the car before the neighbors start asking questions we don't have answers for.

MORTY: We never have answers for anything!

RICK: Exactly why we gotta leave *now*.

They climb in. The car sputters, then roars to life with an unnatural hum.
