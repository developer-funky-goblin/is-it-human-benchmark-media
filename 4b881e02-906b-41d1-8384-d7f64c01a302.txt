Yes, CO2 emissions are a serious problem for several reasons:

1. **Greenhouse effect**: CO2 traps heat in the atmosphere, contributing to global warming and climate change.
2. **Ocean acidification**: Roughly a quarter of emitted CO2 is absorbed by oceans, lowering pH and harming marine ecosystems like coral reefs.
3. **Extreme weather**: Rising global temperatures are linked to more frequent and intense storms, droughts, and heatwaves.
4. **Sea level rise**: Warming causes glacial melt and thermal expansion of seawater, threatening coastal communities.
5. **Agricultural disruption**: Shifting climate patterns can reduce crop yields and threaten food security in vulnerable regions.
6. **Long atmospheric lifetime**: CO2 can persist in the atmosphere for centuries, meaning today's emissions have effects long into the future.

Addressing CO2 emissions requires a combination of reducing fossil fuel use, transitioning to renewable energy, improving energy efficiency, and protecting natural carbon sinks like forests.
