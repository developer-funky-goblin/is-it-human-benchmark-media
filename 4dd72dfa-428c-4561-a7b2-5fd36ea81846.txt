1. **Botanical Name:** *Mentha x piperita 'Strawberry Mint'*
2. **Common Name:** Strawberry Mint
3. **Family:** Lamiaceae (Mint family)
4. **Origin:** A hybrid cultivar of peppermint, bred for its fruity aroma
5. **Growth Habit:** Herbaceous perennial with a spreading, low-growing habit
6. **Height:** 12-24 inches (30-60 cm)
7. **Spread:** 18-36 inches (45-90 cm), spreading via rhizomes
8. **Foliage:** Bright green, serrated leaves with a strong strawberry-mint fragrance
9. **Flowers:** Small, pale purple flowers in whorled spikes, bloom in summer
10. **Hardiness Zones:** USDA zones 3-11 (perennial in zones 5-9)
11. **Sunlight:** Full sun to partial shade
12. **Soil:** Moist, well-drained soil rich in organic matter
13. **Watering:** Regular watering, keeping soil consistently moist but not waterlogged
14. **Propagation:** Easily propagated by division, cuttings, or layering
15. **Uses:** Culinary (teas, desserts, garnishes), aromatic (potpourri, sachets), medicinal (digestive aid)
16. **Care Tips:** Pinch back regularly to encourage bushy growth and prevent legginess; contain spread with barriers or grow in pots
17. **Pests/Diseases:** Generally pest-resistant; watch for rust or powdery mildew in humid conditions
