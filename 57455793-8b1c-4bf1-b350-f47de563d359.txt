Here's a simple recipe for ginger-glazed ham:

1. **Gather Ingredients:** A bone-in ham, fresh ginger, brown sugar, honey, soy sauce, and Dijon mustard.
2. **Preheat Oven:** Set to 325°F (163°C).
3. **Score the Ham:** Cut a diamond pattern into the fat, about 1/4 inch deep.
4. **Make the Glaze:** Whisk together grated ginger, brown sugar, honey, soy sauce, and mustard in a saucepan; simmer until slightly thickened.
5. **Apply Glaze:** Brush half the glaze over the scored ham.
6. **Bake:** Place in the oven, basting with remaining glaze every 20 minutes, until internal temperature reaches 140°F.
7. **Rest and Serve:** Let the ham rest for 10-15 minutes before carving.
