In my December 2023 review I wrote about how We don't yet know how to build GPT-4 -- OpenAI's best model was almost a year old at that point, yet no other AI lab had produced anything better. What did OpenAI know that the rest of us didn't? I'm relieved that this has changed completely in the past twelve months. 18 organizations now have models on the Chatbot Arena Leaderboard that rank higher than the original GPT-4 from March 2023 (GPT-4-0314 on the board) -- 70 models in total.
