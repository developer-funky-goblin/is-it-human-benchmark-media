Judo, meaning "the gentle way," is built on seven core tenets that guide both technique and character:

1. **Courtesy (Rei):** Respect for training partners, instructors, and the dojo itself.
2. **Courage (Yuki):** The willingness to face challenges and take calculated risks.
3. **Honesty (Makoto):** Sincerity in both technique and interaction with others.
4. **Honor (Meiyo):** Upholding one's principles even under pressure.
5. **Modesty (Kenson):** Avoiding arrogance regardless of skill level.
6. **Respect (Sonchou):** Valuing others' efforts and perspectives.
7. **Self-Control (Jisei):** Mastery over one's emotions and impulses, especially during randori.

These principles, formalized by Judo's founder Jigoro Kano, extend beyond the mat into everyday conduct, reflecting Judo's dual purpose as both a martial art and a philosophy of personal development.
