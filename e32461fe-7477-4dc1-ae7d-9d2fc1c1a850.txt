This is the **Zhaoming213/ChatGPT-Corpus** on Hugging Face -- a collection of real conversation logs exported from ChatGPT sessions, formatted as structured JSON with alternating user/assistant turns. It's useful for studying conversational patterns, prompt styles, and assistant response structure across a wide range of real user interactions, though as with any scraped corpus it's worth checking for duplicate or low-quality sessions before using it for training or evaluation.
